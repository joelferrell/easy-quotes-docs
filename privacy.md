# Privacy policy

The full policy lives on the publisher's site, so there is only ever one
authoritative version of it:

**[canonicalscale.com/legal](https://canonicalscale.com/legal)**

That page covers collection, use, sharing, security, retention and deletion, and
it is the URL to quote if a customer or a reviewer asks where the policy is.

## The short version, for merchants

- **Easy Quotes is a processor for your shoppers' data.** You decide what your
  quote form asks for and what happens to the answers; we store them so you can
  act on the quote and pass them to Shopify to create the draft order.
- **Uninstalling deletes your data.** Shopify tells us 48 hours after an
  uninstall, and we erase everything held for the store — quotes, answers,
  forms, translations and settings.
- **Your integration credentials are encrypted at rest.** Slack, Klaviyo and
  HubSpot keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored. See
  [Advanced features](/advanced-features/).
- **Attachments go into your own Shopify Files.** We never keep a copy of the
  contents.
- **Nothing is sent to an integration you have not switched on**, using your own
  credentials.
- **You can delete any quote yourself** from the Quotes page. That removes it
  from Easy Quotes and leaves the Shopify draft order alone.

Questions about any of this, or a request to access or delete personal
information: [help@canonicalscale.com](mailto:help@canonicalscale.com). We
answer within 30 days.
