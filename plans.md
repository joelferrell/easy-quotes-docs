# Plans

Billed through Shopify, so charges appear on your normal Shopify invoice. Paid
plans include a **7-day free trial**, and development stores are free.

## Free — $0

Where a store lands when a trial ends. **Shoppers can't send new quote requests
until you pick a plan**, but nothing is lost:

- Every quote you've already received stays in the app
- Draft orders in Shopify are untouched
- Your form and settings are saved, ready for when you upgrade

## Base — $25.99/month

Everything one store needs to take quote requests:

- Unlimited quote requests
- One quote form
- Text, paragraph, email, phone, number and dropdown fields
- Sections and validation
- A draft order per quote, with the shopper's answers attached
- Add to quote button, quote page and mini quote drawer
- Quote dashboard: conversion, sales and open quote totals

Not included: more than one form, mini quote styles beyond the slide-in drawer,
more than one language, conditional fields, cart-to-quote, marketing opt-ins,
radio and checkbox fields, and Klaviyo.

## Pro — $90/month

Everything in Base, plus:

- Up to 25 quote forms — trade vs. retail, per collection, per market
- Radio buttons, checkboxes and checkbox groups
- Conditional fields and sections
- Email and SMS marketing opt-ins, subscribed in Shopify
- Mini quote styles: the panel under your header link, and the one that pushes
  the page across
- Convert cart to quote
- Webhooks to Zapier, Make or your own endpoint
- Klaviyo events and custom notifications
- Multi-language forms
- Analytics to `dataLayer` and `gtag()`
- Dedicated onboarding and setup

## Changing plan

Your form and settings are kept whatever plan you're on. Downgrading doesn't
delete a Pro-only setting — it just stops applying it, and it comes back if you
upgrade again.
